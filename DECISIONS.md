# Implementation decisions

## Version 0.5 fixes

- Final Medium benchmark retains the existing Intel UHD render-resolution tradeoff: 1920×1080 viewport, 1056×594 internal, six cars on canyon_gp, 67.70 FPS average / 71.94 median, 2.4ms median CPU, maximum 214 calls / 366,403 triangles. Browser suites run sequentially and close each context; the process check found one automated headless Chrome instance. Full-viewport car QA explicitly clears the garage stage's phone zoom/view offset before measuring fixed-distance LOD levels. The result screenshot waits for reveal animations rather than capturing its initially hidden panels.

- The 8m downward corridor ray necessarily meets an intentional tunnel roof or finish gantry before Road. Those named overhead fixtures are accepted only with at least 3m clear headroom; lower geometry still fails, and the 1m horizontal ray has no such exemption. The 24-track scan measured minimum overhead clearance 4.06m. All other objects, including terrain, sea, props and bridge decks, must yield Road/curb/decal as the first hit. Background ridges were a separate visual fault outside Track.root: the old fixed 700m ring intersected GP tracks. They now follow the camera at background depth, like the sky dome; all 120 chase frames were repeated and inspected after that fix.

- Road decals retain the requested grid/UV cells but split them along the Road mesh's own triangle edges. Sampling only Track.at() on a 6×6 grid floated up to 60mm above a tight reversed neon_c bank because the road is already triangulated. Exact triangle clipping passes 50 deterministic random raycasts per actual patch on all 24 track/direction combinations (5–50mm vertical clearance), including the start seam. Light pools crop at the asphalt edge, retain their original radial UV centre/tints and merge into one mesh. Dead flat marking geometry was removed; the existing road shader already paints markings directly on Road.

## Version 0.3 execution

- Task 6: the final checklist asks for a bridge and tunnel on every GP, but the supplied `canyon_gp` lists neither. Its final coordinates are unchanged; sections at t=0.34–0.41 (bridge) and t=0.89–0.94 (tunnel) complete the stated checklist. A heightfield cannot represent the empty space under a tunnel roof: the road corridor stays carved below its surface, the terrain rises outside the corridor, and an independent arch forms the roof. Filling the heightfield above the lane blocked the AI at the portal; the carved corridor passes all three GP laps without resets. Audio sources are untouched.

- Task 5: the exact x2.4 legacy coordinates are retained. The current `neon_c` implementation measures 10.92 m minimum radius after scaling with its old Catmull-Rom tension, below the brief's 14 m rule (the brief's predicted 15.6 m does not match this code). Only that layout uses centripetal smoothing, yielding 15.86 m with the same scaled points. All tracks now share the actual 3D sample/width geometry contract; roadside clearance deliberately projects horizontally so tall scenery cannot pass solely because its centre is high above the asphalt.

- Task 4 sequencing: the generic roadside clearance formula cannot apply to the boundary wall itself (its centre is exactly halfWidth + 0.8), painted curbs, tokens, or overhead gantry beams. These are intentional track fixtures with separate alignment/post checks; genuine scenery receives no exemption. The unscaled legacy neon_c radius is smaller than its half-width. Task 4 checks roadside objects and the existing guarded wall line; the strict no-fold/minimum-radius acceptance follows the explicitly ordered x2.4 scaling in Task 5, rather than silently changing the road width or scaling early.

- Task 3: Rapier 0.21's `FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES` rejects reverse-face contacts on open ribbons. Walls also enable its documented `FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES_TWO_SIDED` flag, retaining a single continuous mesh per side. Rapier exposes persistent manifold contacts even when solver-contact count is zero, so wall glide transforms local contact points as a fallback. Existing audio has a one-argument `sfx` API; scrapes reuse `impact` at most every 0.4 seconds without modifying audio code or its mixer design.

- Task 1: `Controls.steer` is screen-relative (+right); the vehicle converts once to world yaw. AI returns the screen convention and network/wheel steering remains world-relative. The handling metrics' drift feedback also converts its world slip sign at the controller boundary; the requested right-key binding remains unchanged. This preserves the measured drift magnitude instead of measuring the wrong feedback direction.

- Build the milestones in specification order, with TypeScript builds between stages. The workspace starts without a Git repository; milestones are recorded here and in PROGRESS.md rather than creating commits in a nonexistent repository.
- Blender is absent from the command line. Use specification §6.6's deterministic Three.js procedural fallback with the specified node names. No third-party car models, brands, imagery, fonts, or audio are needed.
- Use metres, +Y up, +Z forward, a 60 Hz fixed physics step, and Rapier's actual four-wheel raycast vehicle controller for player and opponents.
- All art and audio are generated locally. A procedural PMREM studio environment provides reflections without remote HDRI downloads. Advanced SSR/CSM/DOF remain feature-flagged until implemented and measured.
- Save schema v1 is validated and recovered without crashing. Buying, upgrades and cosmetics modify this local save only.
- Postprocessing uses linear half-float buffers and a final ACES ToneMappingEffect. SMAA and chromatic aberration occupy separate passes because their convolution effects cannot be merged. N8AO is off in Low and uses half resolution in Medium.
- Auto quality benchmarks frames for 2.5 seconds at the start of each race and lowers resolution/shadow/AO cost below 48 FPS. Physical-device performance acceptance remains separate from headless functional testing.

- Task 7 font decision: the brief explicitly requires bundled Barlow Condensed and Inter. These font packages use SIL OFL 1.1, not CC0; the named-font instruction takes precedence over its generic asset restriction. Their original licenses remain bundled. No external art assets added.
- Task 7 mode strip: the code has six game modes. The seventh tab is the existing Daily Challenge, preserving all existing modes and daily logic without inventing a new mode.

- Task 11 performance decision: this host exposes Intel UHD (ANGLE D3D11). Native 1920×1080 Medium with N8AO measured 16.6 FPS; removing redundant car shadow casters and separate reflector draws reduced the scene below 400 calls. On Intel HD/UHD only, Medium caps internal render width at 1056 pixels and disables AO while retaining 1024 soft shadows, bloom, SMAA, grading and native-resolution HTML HUD. High/Ultra retain N8AO and full resolution. Measured result at the required 1920×1080 viewport: 67.1 FPS, 260 calls, 319,590 triangles. This is a render-resolution tradeoff, not a claim of native-1080p 60 FPS. Other GPUs keep the existing Medium resolution.
- Wheel geometry is instanced in left/right pairs so the four named steering/spin nodes remain compatible with local, remote and replay animations.

- Task 12 lobby decision: WebRTC diagnostics confirmed ICE worked, but periodic ping re-renders could detach CONNECT between pointer-down/up. Game's lobby adapter now compares roster/config excluding ping and updates only ping text for ping-only events. Peer protocol, codec, ICE servers and src/net remain unchanged.
- GP Free Roam uses six alternating-side exits on terrain sections, 30 tokens, five traps and four ramps. Reverse civilian traffic bypasses sub-14m sections rather than meeting forward cars in a one-way chicane. Optional Sprint was deferred as permitted by the brief.

- VELOX0.6 precedence:the explicit C3/H2/H5/H6 text sizes and B3/H6 gaps take precedence over generic type/spacing rules. H4 permits only additive hintRaces/showControlHints settings;old progress and schema version remain intact.
- VELOX0.6 scrolling QA:wheel scroll-behavior is now auto;programmatic reveal explicitly requests smooth/nearest or instant for reduced motion. Profile labels now precede values per I3. Garage size checks allow0.01px browser subpixel rounding.
- VELOX0.6 I8 exception:Computer Use stopped because Chrome URL confidence was insufficient. No further native GUI input was issued;CDP trace and PerformanceObserver cover41 known-localhost UI actions. Audio initialization and race/shader warm-up retain measured tasks over100ms because the brief protects those subsystems. Exact durations and closest implementation are disclosed in CODEX_REPORT_3.md.
